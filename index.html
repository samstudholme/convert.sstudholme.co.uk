    Frequently asked questions

    Do my files stay on my device?

    Yes. Conversion happens locally in your browser, so your files are never uploaded.

    Which conversions are supported?

    HEIC, HEIF or WebP to JPG or PNG; JPG, PNG, HEIC, DOCX or compatible Pages files to PDF; WAV to MP3; FLAC to WAV; and MOV to MP4.

    Does it work on mobile?

    Yes. Modern mobile browsers are supported. Large documents, audio and video files may take longer and require more available memory.
